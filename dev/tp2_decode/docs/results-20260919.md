## What the counters support

ACU uses forced-cold per-kernel replay, not the rotating-event regime above.
Durations from its producer and reducer must not replace complete-call timing.
Stall-per-issue ratios are not percentages of elapsed time.

- Q8 shared down: DRAM reads3.370→3.356MB, L1/L2 traffic27.101→7.010MB
  (−74.13%), vector loads30720→13824 (−55%). Achieved occupancy falls
  15.94→8.25% while the complete call gets33.69% faster. This supports lower
  on-chip request/instruction overhead, not fewer stored weight bytes.
- Q8 shared projection: L1/L2 traffic27.658→14.197MB and vector loads
  30720→14592, again with effectively unchanged DRAM reads.
- Q8 output: producer L1/L2 traffic55.007→28.055MB. The S8 reducer adds
  0.111MB of L1/L2 traffic and is included in the12.083us event result.
- Q8 paired shared chain takes the opposite tradeoff: CTAs64→128,
  L1/L2 traffic14.183→27.782MB, achieved occupancy10.91→21.43%, dependency
  stall ratio1.603→0.314. More parallel work wins here despite higher traffic;
  the unfused hoist recipe must not blindly replace the paired recipe.
- Q4 projection: nearly unchanged DRAM/L1-L2 traffic and vector-load count,
  registers64→58. The fixed address/index path is the useful hypothesis,
  not improved weight coalescing. The paired kernel remains94 registers with
  very little measured improvement; its different mapping needs separate work.
- Q6 candidate removes recorded shared-bank conflicts and reduces registers
  114→74, but vector loads increase1,590,800→4,656,000 and the full call loses.
  Better occupancy or one improved counter is insufficient for promotion.

PP2048/TG128, request batch1: native12.724309ms/token, K-pack12.482082ms/token
from `kpack-tp2.om9JQj.results.tgz`. This component archive contains no TPOT.

That model's saved M1 plan contains397 distinct local tensor operations per
rank: QKV36, attention gate36, attention Q12, output48, shared gate/up96,

The two TP ranks duplicate these plans; their durations must not be summed

- Picking each ordinary GEMV's minimum confirmed time (including old TC)
  saves963.138us/token in a component-sum model.
- The M1 prepare delta contributes127.140us/token more.
- Subtraction from the old TPOT gives11.391804ms/token, an8.735% reduction.

critical-path overlap, launch costs and PCCL are not remeasured here.
