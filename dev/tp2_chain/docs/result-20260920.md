# Paired chain result review, 2026-09-20

The M1 TP2-local chain has a measured winner: `paired-1`, the C4/W4/P4/S1
projection reader with a paired finish. It reduces the complete chain from
43.366286 to 39.847999 us (-8.112954%). The existing 35B paired C4/W8/P8/S1
implementation remains the best 35B choice. This review does not change
production selection or publish replacement model libraries.

## Complete-chain timing

M=1, E=256, top-k=8, BF16 compute. N below is the logical width of each gate
or up; merged physical gate/up has 2N rows. Same router/prepare, Q5 down and
weighted finish in every arm. No newly optimized Q5 down candidate was mixed
into this causal comparison.

| Point | Unfused | Shipped paired | Historical paired clone | Projection-reader paired |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| TP2, N512/K3072 | 43.366286 | 45.222856 | 45.042285 | **39.847999** |
| 35B, N512/K2048 | 36.983000 | **30.855499** | 30.880001 | 33.840001 |

All times are microseconds per complete chain, not isolated producer time.
The TP2 candidate beats the unfused incumbent in all six paired rounds,
with per-round changes from -8.937912% to -7.183615%. Its absolute saving is
3.518287 us per call. Both old paired TP2 arms lose in all six rounds.

On 35B, the new candidate is 9.672511% slower than the already-shipping paired
winner. Its apparent 8.498497% improvement against unfused is not a production
improvement. The historical fixed clone differs by only +0.079409% from the
shipping implementation; retain shipping.

The TP2 ring contains seven copies of 22,806,528 active weight bytes per call
(159,645,696 bytes). The 35B ring contains ten copies of 15,204,352 bytes
(152,043,520 bytes). Both exceed 2.25x L2. Complete graph traversals contain
35/40 chains respectively; first replay is excluded. This controls weight
residency, not every cache input or real-model behavior.

Modeled full-chain weight MBU at the user's 2700 GB/s roof rises from 19.48%
to 21.20% on TP2. The retained 35B paired chain is 18.25%. These include
router/activation/finish latency and are not individual GEMV DRAM throughput
counters or proof that the 40%/60% optimization goals are reached.

ACU uses kernel replay with cache control; these instrumented timings are
separate from the rotating complete-chain timing above. Do not replace the
measured chain time with a sum of these rows.

There are exactly five versus four kernels. The fused producer adds only
0.423529 us relative to gate/up alone while removing the separate activation.
The gate/up-plus-activation region falls from 23.142941 to 18.456471 us.
Prepare/down/finish code and geometry are unchanged; variations in their
profile replay times are not evidence of new implementations or independent
optimization wins.

The traffic is almost unchanged, so this is primarily an activation-fusion
win, not a new global-memory coalescing improvement. The paired body preserves
the fast mantissa extraction / F32 affine/FMA reader. The extra 2,048 vector
loads are consistent with its upstream-status guard (one per warp across
512 blocks x four warps); that causal explanation is a source/counter
inference, not a separate guard-ablation experiment.

For 35B, the retained paired producer is 14.932941 us versus 16.970000 us
unfused gate/up plus 5.124118 us activation. It uses 256 blocks of 256 threads,
94 registers and 1024 shared bytes. L1/L2 traffic is 18,565,888 versus
35,469,184 bytes, with near-equal DRAM reads (9,482,112 / 9,494,016 bytes).
That is the already-existing paired reader benefit; it is not a new gain to
count again in projected TPOT.

Metric definitions were checked in SDK `MemoryWorkloadAnalysis_Tables.section`:
`derived__l2_bytes_pipe_l1_total` is L1/L2 global load/store/atomic traffic,
not unique DRAM reads. Stall ratios are per active issue, not wall-time shares.
The reports warn ICN collection is disabled; no interconnect claim is made.

## Decision and next handoff

1. Integrate only the measured TP2 M1 Q4 paired reader into the existing paired
   dispatch path: logical N512/K3072, E256/top8, F32 endpoints/BF16 compute,
   C4/W4/P4/S1, Changes=3, fixed N/K, paired-N4 finish. Preserve its BF16
   projection rounding and original-slot down/finish contract.
2. Keep the 35B paired C4/W8/P8 winner and current unmeasured-shape fallbacks.
   M=2…8 correctness here does not establish their best performance choices.
3. Retain the frozen Q5 down during initial model integration. Its previous
   P4-fixed winner is a separate admission/causal change, not part of this
   measured fusion improvement.
4. Rebuild only affected small integration components, then run a warmed
   model A/B and Asys to prove actual paired selection, removed activation and
   unchanged downstream routing/finish. This archive has no new model TPOT.

At 47–48 eligible serial MoE layers, 3.518287 us/call corresponds to roughly
0.165–0.169 ms/token before overlap and scheduling effects. It is a component
projection, not a guaranteed model saving. Do not add the two TP ranks: their
work is parallel. The previous warmed native TPOT was about 11.23 ms, so this
fusion alone is not a multi-millisecond end-to-end improvement.

Current production files, artifact pins, and immutable build receipts remain
unchanged by this review.
