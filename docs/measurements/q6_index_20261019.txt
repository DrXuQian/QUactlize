Why this candidate, and what counts as success
--------------------------------------------
The uploaded A2 focus report (M8/N512/K2048/E256/topk8/channels8/cluster)
still executed 4677632 v.shrl operations. 1245184 shift executions use
26..31-bit operands; surrounding native code connects many of these to
signed index division/modulo corrections. Integer-pipe activity is about
62%, so eliminating the corrections is plausible, but not proof that they
are on the latency critical path. They may overlap memory service.

An unsigned outer loop alone is insufficient: int k and signed word_index/
word_slot parameters turn the coordinate back into a signed value. A3 keeps
it unsigned through these helpers and retains uint64_t address products.
Negative expert IDs, signed Q6 scales and the public validator are untouched.

The user's score is CE elapsed cycles, NOT converted microseconds or static
instruction totals. Historical A1 vs A2 reports have 112833 vs 108478 cycles
but different reported cycle rates, 1.0 vs 1.7 GHz. Their 112.833 vs 63.811 us
durations therefore do not prove a 43% source-level speedup. Nor is the
3.86% cross-run cycle difference a fresh same-build paired result.

Box experiment and decision
---------------------------
tools/run_q6_index_box.sh defaults to one focus point, one configuration,
two arms. It retains independent GGUF, RAW equality, changed-input, large-BF16,
zero-A, negative-control and guard checks. Cold rotating event timings run
separately as secondary evidence, not the cycle selection score.

ACU order: A2,A3 / A3,A2 / A2,A3. Each capture performs one direct kernel,
one weight copy, no CUDA Graph and no cold-ring timing loop inside ACU.
Profiler cache-control=all. Record same-device/build/workload/geometry plus
CE/CU cycles and clock fields, registers, traffic and instruction counters.
Each process rechecks source, compiler/runtime, library and physical device.
Mixed pairs or mismatched clock conditions cannot certify a speedup. Even
LOWER_CYCLES_ALL_PAIRS remains scoped evidence requiring variance/counter
review; performance_admitted stays false. If cycles do not decrease
repeatably without numeric regression, reject/revise A3 despite fewer shifts.

POINTS=all adds the original seven numerical/event controls; it does not turn
one-point profiling into multi-shape cycle admission. No broad sweep needed.
Upload the printed results/review.tgz, including all six .acurep files.
Current status: DEVICE_CORRECTNESS_NOT_RUN, CYCLE_GAIN_NOT_RUN,
PRODUCTION_UNCHANGED. Do not use this record as a shipping selector result.
